# The Bard

A minstrel and co-op support character.

- **Song** cards start a Song (a power whose number is its **Verses**). Only one Song plays at a time:
  starting a new one ends the old one and triggers its **Finale**.
- Every non-Song card you play adds a Verse (max 15).
- **Refrain**: at the start of your turn the Song helps ALL players.
- **Finale**: when the Song ends, a big effect that scales with Verses.
- Upgrading a Song card makes it start with more Verses (1 → 3).

| Song | Rarity | Refrain (each turn) | Finale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Battle Hymn | Basic | ALL players gain 3 Block | 2 damage to ALL enemies per Verse |
| Jig | Common | Draw 1 card | 1 Energy per 5 Verses |
| Ballad of Valor | Uncommon | ALL players gain 2 Vigor | ALL players gain 2 Block per Verse |
| Lullaby | Uncommon | 1 Weak to ALL enemies | 1 Vulnerable to ALL enemies per 3 Verses |
| Requiem | Rare (2) | 3 damage to ALL enemies | 4 damage to ALL enemies per Verse |

Starter: 4 Strike, 4 Defend, Battle Hymn, Hum. Relic **Worn Lute**: your first Song each combat starts with
3 extra Verses. HP 70.
